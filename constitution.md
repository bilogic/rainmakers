## 1. Shared Vision/Purpose and Values

Our shared vision/purpose is to become better people by sharing our personal and business lives in an atmosphere of confidentiality, trust, respect and intimacy. We commit to ourselves and to each other the time, resources and opportunity to achieve the personal, professional and spiritual growth we desire. Our values include trust, transparency and openness.

## 2. Fiscal Year

1 June to 30 May or as per YEN’s calendar

## 3. Confidentiality

Any breach of confidentiality will result in immediate expulsion from the Forum. After a Forum discussion (impact, apology, etc.), the offending member may be readmitted only by unanimous vote.

Fine of <span style="color:#FFFF00">[SGD50/-]</span> after <span style="color:#FFFF00">[30 min]</span> grace period from start time and an additional <span style="color:#FFFF00">[½]</span> absence will be assessed if a member arrives <span style="color:#FFFF00">[1 hour]</span> or later but before end time.

**Meeting Dates**

Forum members will set the time, date and location of meetings;

Respond to requests to set forum dates promptly; silence will be construed as agreement; dates can be set six months to one year in advance

## 5. Forum Protocol

**Respectful Interaction**

Members commit to treating each other with dignity and respect during forum discussions

**Effective Forum Practice**

Planning and Pacing: Members are encouraged not to rush and to plan ahead for meaningful contributions and to be able to extract personal value from each session

Time Regulation: A timer will be employed to regulate forum sharing and activities.

Advance Scheduling: Locking in forum dates well in advance (6 months to 1 year) to maximize attendance

Mindful Exploration: Members will use the forum to calm the mind and explore the Johari unknown quadrant

**Rule Adherence and Closure**

Upholding Principles: Rules are in place to uphold forum principles and provide closure.

Collective Comfort: Encouragement for all members to follow the rules, ensuring a comfortable environment for everyone.

Closure for Perspective: Closure is implemented to prevent unnecessary comparisons with past incidents.

**Respectful Listening**

Members are obligated to pay attention and refrain from disrupting when another member is speaking. This includes both verbal and non-verbal cues such as noise or actions.

**Food/Alcohol**

Members are reminded to consume alcohol moderately, avoiding any state of intoxication during and prior to meetings. Drinking during retreats is permitted only at designated times. While food is available, it should not detract from our Forum experience. Respectful behavior, particularly refraining from intoxication, is expected when members are speaking.

## 6. Emergencies and Unforeseen Schedule Changes

**Adherence to Agreed Forum Schedule**

In general, the agreed-upon forum date, start time, and end time will be observed. Any proposed adjustments must obtain unanimous agreement from all attendees, considering the collective impact.

**Duration Norms**

As a standard, the forum duration will be an average of 30 minutes per participant, totaling 3.5 hours for a group of 7 participants. While the forum may conclude earlier, such expectations should not be presumed.

**Absence Protocol**

In the event of non-attendance due to reasons such as being in an unfit state of mind or a family emergency, members are required to communicate their circumstances clearly and directly to the group. Additionally, if possible, an explanation of the situation should be provided.

## 7. Membership and Voting

**Group Size**

Our forum will have 7 - 9 members

**Members Joining Forum**

Members joining the Forum must meet majority consent of the existing members.

**Business Dealings**

All business transactions more than <span style="color:#FFFF00">[US$100,000]</span> are strongly discouraged among Forum members and must be disclosed to the Forum.

**Romantic Relationships**

No romantic relationships are permitted among Forum members.

**Voting**

Members can vote on new rules, waiver or alternatives of similar value to fines

Voting must be in person + quorum of 50%, moderator/longest forum membership decide if draw
