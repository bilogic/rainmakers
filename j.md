# Y.E.N. Rainmakers Forum Constitution

Draft for discussion, revised from the 28 October 2026 draft. Every substantive change is listed in the Appendix and needs the forum's agreement before signing.

## 1. Purpose, Mission and Values

**1.1 Purpose.** The Y.E.N. Rainmakers Forum (the "Forum") is a group of individuals committed to building a close bond and a secure, confidential space in which each member can grow personally and professionally, and enjoy the journey together.

**1.2 Mission.** To help each member become a better version of themselves, together.

**1.3 Values.** Growth, Respect, Openness, Fun and Trust (GROFT). Members commit to:

- share their personal and business lives openly within this confidential environment;
- challenge one another, respectfully, to grow; and
- strengthen the Forum's bonds through shared experiences.

## 2. Definitions

**2.1 Year.** The Forum year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Attendance, absences, fines, dues and role terms are all measured over this year.

**2.2 Meeting.** A scheduled forum meeting, including the annual retreat. The Forum holds ten meetings per year.

**2.3 Absence.** Non-attendance at a meeting, other than attendance by approved video or teleconference under clause 5.4.

**2.4 Member in good standing.** A current member who is not subject to removal proceedings under these norms.

**2.5 Unanimous.** Agreement of all members in good standing, excluding any member who is the subject of, or has a personal interest in, the decision.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

**3.1 Elected roles.** The Moderator, Deputy Moderator, Treasurer and Retreat Chair are elected by vote at the first meeting of each year and serve a twelve-month term. Outgoing office-holders hand over at that meeting.

**3.2 Moderator.** Runs meetings effectively; maintains the attendance register, updated after each meeting and visible to all members; maintains the parking lot; and runs the annual Forum Health Survey.

**3.3 Deputy Moderator.** Supports the Moderator and chairs meetings in the Moderator's absence. The Deputy Moderator is the presumptive nominee for Moderator the following year, subject to the annual vote.

**3.4 Treasurer.** Manages the Forum kitty: collects dues, contributions and fines; pays meeting-room and shared food and beverage costs; and circulates a short statement after each meeting.

**3.5 Retreat Chair.** Leads the retreat committee and delivers the retreat within the agreed budget.

**3.6 Meeting roles.** For each meeting the Moderator appoints a Protocol Officer, who evaluates the Forum's use of Forum Protocol.

**3.7 Moderator training.** The Moderator and Deputy Moderator attend a Forum Moderator and Leadership Development workshop before their term begins.

**3.8 Training standard.** Each member completes Forum Fundamentals training within twelve months of joining. At all times, at least half of the members are Fundamentals-trained and at least a quarter are Moderator-trained.

## 4. Confidentiality

**4.1 Nothing, Nobody, Never.** Everything shared in the Forum is confidential, permanently. Members do not disclose Forum content to anyone outside the Forum, including spouses, family and colleagues.

**4.2 Permission.** Content may be shared outside the Forum only with the express permission of the member who shared it.

**4.3 Within the Forum.** Forum matters may be discussed outside meetings only in private settings, and only among members.

**4.4 Required disclosure.** Clause 4.1 does not apply where disclosure is required by law, regulation or court order, or is reasonably necessary to prevent serious harm to any person. A member who makes such a disclosure informs the Moderator as soon as lawfully possible.

**4.5 Breaches.** A suspected breach is reported to the Moderator. The Moderator informs the member concerned and gives them the opportunity to respond before the Forum. If the Forum, acting unanimously, finds that a breach occurred, the member resigns or is removed.

**4.6 Supplementary rules.** No separate confidentiality agreement is required; signing these norms constitutes each member's undertaking. Where these norms are silent, the established YEN Forum confidentiality rules apply.

## 5. Commitment and Attendance

**5.1 Expectation.** Members attend every meeting. Attendance of at least 80% (eight of ten meetings) is the minimum standard. Attendance for the full retreat is compulsory.

**5.2 Notice.** A member advises the Moderator before any absence, as early as possible.

**5.3 Consequences of absence.** Within any year:

| Absence | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| First | No penalty |
| Second | SGD 200 fine |
| Third | Automatic removal from the Forum and all Forum channels |

**5.4 Remote attendance.** A member may attend up to two meetings per year by video or teleconference; these count as attendance. The retreat cannot be attended remotely.

**5.5 Reinstatement.** A member removed under clause 5.3 may be reinstated only by unanimous vote.

**5.6 Punctuality.** Meetings start and end on time. Members aim to arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled start. A member who arrives after the scheduled start is given a grace period of 30 minutes, a flat SGD 50 fine shall apply thereafter, paid directly to the treasurer.

**5.7 Devices.** Phones and other devices are switched off or surrendered for the duration of the meeting.

## 6. Meetings and Scheduling

**6.1 Calendar.** Members fix all meeting dates for the coming year at the start of each year, in the manner of a board calendar.

**6.2 Quorum.** Quorum is 70% of current members, rounded up (seven of ten; six of eight or nine).

**6.3 Inquorate meetings.** If quorum cannot be met and no alternative date can be found before the next scheduled meeting, the meeting is cancelled and does not count toward attendance.

**6.4 Rescheduling.** Changing a confirmed date is strongly discouraged. A change takes effect only with the unanimous agreement of the members able to attend the original date; otherwise the original date stands. The member requesting the change runs a poll, first exploring a different time on the same day, then alternative dates.

**6.5 Emergency meetings.** Any member may call an emergency meeting and chairs it. Attendance is voluntary and does not count toward the attendance register.

## 7. Forum Protocol

**7.1** Members treat one another with dignity and respect.

**7.2** Members speak from personal experience and do not give advice unless asked.

**7.3** The Forum follows Forum Protocol, and the Processor evaluates its use at each meeting.

## 8. Membership

**8.1 Size.** The Forum has eight to ten members.

**8.2 Conflicts of interest.** Members may not have a conflict of interest with one another, meaning a competing business, a material commercial relationship, or a close personal relationship that could inhibit open sharing. A member who foresees a potential conflict raises it with the Forum early. If a conflict arises, the member whose circumstances created it resigns and is referred to a conflict-free forum when one is available.

**8.3 Admission.** New members join in groups of at least two, no more than once every two years, one to two meetings before the retreat. If membership falls below eight, the Forum may admit members outside this cycle. Admission requires a unanimous vote.

**8.4 Admission process.**

- The Moderator informs the Forum of interested candidates.
- Members raise any conflicts of interest or objections.
- Candidates join current members for a meal.
- Candidates receive these norms and the meeting calendar for review.
- Following a unanimous vote, the Moderator confirms the candidate's acceptance and joining date.

**8.5 Onboarding.** Each new member is assigned a Forum mentor, meets every current member one-to-one within twelve months, and buys into the kitty pro rata.

**8.6 Renewal and exit.** Membership is renewed annually; a decision not to renew is respected. A member leaving mid-year informs the Moderator with at least two months' notice where possible.

**8.7 Leave.** The Forum does not grant sabbaticals or leaves of absence. A member wishing to step away exits and may re-apply as a new member.

**8.8 Readmission after removal.** A member removed for a breach of these norms may apply for readmission, which requires unanimous agreement after discussion.

## 9. Meeting Structure, Meals and Costs

**9.1 Structure.** Meetings follow the recommended forum meeting structure.

**9.2 Forum dinners.** Dinners after official meetings are encouraged but optional. The target cost is below SGD 100 per attending member, excluding alcohol.

**9.3 Cost sharing.** Food and non-alcoholic costs are split equally among the members who attend. Alcohol is paid for by those who drink it.

**9.4 Minimum spend.** Where a venue has a minimum spend, that amount is split equally among attending members regardless of alcohol consumption. Alcohol costs above the minimum spend are paid by those who drink it.

**9.5 Transport.** Each member covers their own transport to and from the venue.

## 10. Retreats

**10.1 Status.** The annual retreat counts as a meeting, with the same roles and norms in force. The Forum also aims to hold a mini-retreat when possible.

**10.2 Committee.** A retreat committee of three, led by the Retreat Chair, organises the retreat and draws on YEN resources where available.

**10.3 Budget.** The default budget is SGD 5,000 per person, covering accommodation, food and beverages, shared costs and official activities. Flights, alcohol and optional activities are excluded.

**10.4 Budget revision.** The Moderator or Retreat Chair collects each member's preferred budget anonymously. The budget is set at the lowest figure submitted.

**10.5 Date changes.** Once retreat dates are confirmed, a change requires unanimous agreement, reached at least 60 calendar days before the originally confirmed start date. The member who requests the change becomes Retreat Chair, unless the Forum decides otherwise.

**10.6 Official programme.** Once the itinerary is signed off, participation in all official activities is compulsory. Official hours end after dinner each day.

**10.7 Optional activities.** Drinking, extreme activities and any activity raising safety or religious concerns are always optional, and are paid for separately by those who take part.

**10.8 Uncertainty.** Any question not covered by this section is raised with the Forum for discussion.

## 11. Business Between Members

**11.1 Disclosure.** Members disclose to the Forum any business dealings with another member, or with an entity another member controls.

**11.2 Consent threshold.** Any transaction or series of transactions between members with an aggregate value of SGD 100,000 or more in a year requires unanimous approval before it proceeds. The members involved do not vote.

**11.3 Proceeding without consent.** A member who pursues a transaction that the Forum has not approved must exit the Forum before any funds change hands.

**11.4 Third-party co-investments.** Co-investments controlled and managed by a third party are permitted but discouraged. The opportunity must be offered equally to all members, and clauses 11.1 to 11.3 still apply.

**11.5 No solicitation.** Members do not use Forum meetings to solicit business or investment from one another.

## 12. Forum Health and Renewal

**12.1** The Moderator runs the Forum Health Survey annually to assess the group's health.

**12.2** The Forum engages a Certified Forum Facilitator at least once every three years.

**12.3** The Moderator leads a review of these norms at each annual retreat.

## 13. Finances and Dues

**13.1 Dues.** Annual dues are SGD 200 per member, collected in January. The Treasurer may propose a different amount at the annual retreat, based on the coming year's budget.

**13.2 Fines.** Fines are paid into the kitty within 30 days. The Treasurer records and collects them.

**13.3 Reporting.** The Treasurer circulates a statement of the kitty at the end of every quarter per calendar year.

## 14. Decision-Making and Amendments

**14.1 Unanimous decisions.** The following require a unanimous vote: admission of new members, readmission or reinstatement of a member, findings of a confidentiality breach, approval of transactions under clause 11.2, and changes to confirmed meeting or retreat dates.

**14.2 Amendments.** These norms may be amended with the written consent of all members in good standing except at most one.

**14.3 Other decisions.** All other decisions are made by simple majority of members present at a quorate meeting.

## Signatures

By signing, each member agrees to abide by these norms.
