# Y.E.N. Rainmakers Forum Manifesto & Norms — (For Discussion)

Last updated 28 Oct 2026.

## Forum Manifesto

Conversation starter. Let's take 10 minutes to each think about:

- how we currently view the forum (what are the best parts that we treasure about the forum today)

- how we see ourselves in the forum (or not) in the next 10 years, what role will this forum play in our lives by that specific point in time?

- What would you like to get from the forum to grow in the next 2 years.

## Vision/Purpose and Values

We are a group of individuals with a shared vision of creating a unique bond and secure space that enables us to grow and become better versions of ourselves, while having lots of fun along the way as we journey through life together.

### Mission

To grow and become better versions of ourselves together.

### Values

Our values include growth, respect, openness, fun, trust (GROFT).

We commit to sharing our personal and business lives openly in this unique, confidential and trusted environment. We commit to challenge each other to grow to become better versions of ourselves, respectfully. We strengthen our bonds and have fun through unique experiences together.

## Forum Norms

### ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Annual roles: moderator, deputy moderator and treasurer are selected by blind vote at the beginning of each calendar year; the moderator serves a 12-month term.

Moderator: Runs the forum effectively. Keeps the attendance register. Maintains the parking lot.

Deputy moderator: The moderator’s successor and support. ▸ Replaces the moderator in his absence and, under normal course, becomes moderator the following year.

Treasurer: keeps track of group expenses. ▸ Runs the kitty: member contributions in, meeting-room and shared F&B costs out, with a simple statement shared after each meeting.

Forum Retreat Chair: Organises the retreat effectively. Ensures that retreat stays within budget.

Both moderator and deputy attend a Forum Moderator & Leadership Development workshop before the term begins, or as soon as possible after it starts.

Training health: every member completes Forum fundamentals Training within 12 months of joining; at any time at least half the forum is Fundamentals-trained and at least a quarter moderator-trained.

### CONFIDENTIALITY

Nothing, Nobody, Never: confidentiality is not selective; it is absolute, in all ways and forever. Nothing is discussed with outsiders, except with permission.

Forum business may be discussed outside meetings only in private settings with other forum members.

Suspected breaches will be reported to the moderator. A breach will result in the resignation or expulsion of the person who committed it.

No separate written confidentiality contract is required; where these norms are silent, established YEN Forum confidentiality rules apply.

### COMMITMENT AND ATTENDANCE

We will meet 10 times per year (including retreats); >80% attendance overall is expected. Retreat attendance is compulsory.

Members will  be allowed 1 absence from forum meeting per fiscal year.

Members will be given the option to join forum meetings via teleconference a maximum of twice per fiscal year.

A SGD200 fine shall apply to members who are a no show for a second time per fiscal year.

A third absence from forum meetings in a fiscal year shall trigger an automatic expulsion from the forum and removal from all correspondence channels subject to a unanimous reinstatement vote.

The moderator for the year is responsible for keeping track of attendance. ▸ The moderator keeps a simple attendance register, updated after each meeting and visible to all members.

Members advise the moderator before any absence.

Meetings start and end on time.

Electronic devices are surrendered or off during meetings so members can be fully present.

▸ Punctuality: We start and ebnd on time to respect members time. members plan to arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled start and a subsequent 50$ flat rate fine shall apply thereafter.

### MEETINGS AND SCHEDULING

Meeting dates are set by members at the beginning of each year, one year in advance — similar to how board meetings are scheduled.

Quorum for each meeting is 70% (7 out of 10).

If quorum is not achieved and no alternative date can be found before the next forum meeting, that particular forum meeting is cancelled, and does not count towards the attendance register.

Rescheduling: changing a confirmed date is strongly discouraged. A change takes effect only with unanimous agreement of all members able to attend the original date; otherwise the original date stands.

In the event of a reschedule request, the member in question shall co-ordinate a poll to determine a suitable alternative. The poll shall first explore changes to the original time, followed by alternative dates and times if necessary.

Any member may call an emergency meeting; attendance is voluntary. ▸ The member calling it acts as day chair.

### FORUM PROTOCOL

We treat each other with dignity and respect.

We speak only from experience, rather than giving advice, unless requested.

We practice Forum Protocol, with a processor to evaluate our use of it.

### MEMBERSHIP

Eight to 10 members, with no conflicts of interest.

If a conflict develops, the member whose circumstances created it resigns and is placed in a conflict-free forum when available.

If a member foresees a potential conflict (business or personal), he alerts the forum early rather than waiting for it to crystallise.

New members: at least two at a time, no more than once every two years, admitted one to two meetings before the retreat; each is assigned a forum mentor. The admission vote is unanimous.

New-member process:

▸ The moderator advises the forum of interested candidates.

▸ Members check for conflicts of interest and objections.

▸ Candidates join a meal with current members.

▸ Norms and meeting dates are shared with the candidate for review; the moderator confirms acceptance and joining date.

▸ New members meet each current member 1-to-1 within 12 months of joining, and buy into the kitty pro-rata.

Exiting members: commitment is renewed annually. Non-renewal is possible and respected. Exiting members within the year inform the moderator, striving for at least 2 months notice.

A member removed for a norms violation may apply for readmission; readmission requires the forum's unanimous agreement after discussion, otherwise the removal stands.

No sabbaticals or leaves of absence: a member who wishes to step away temporarily exits and re-applies like any new member.

### MEETING STRUCTURE, FEES AND VENUE

We follow the recommended forum meeting structure, with a scribe capturing feedback for the presenter.

Forum dinners are encouraged but optional. Dinner food cost (excluding alcohol) is split evenly by all members — this applies only to dinners after official forum meetings. All-in dinner cost at SGD 100 per member (excluding alcohol).

All meal costs shall be split equally and alcohol costs will be borne by those who consume it during the meal.

In the event that there is a minimum spend requirement, the base cost shall be split equally regardless of alcohol consumption, subsequent alcohol associated costs over and above the minimum spend shall be borne only by those who consume it.

Each member covers his own transport to and from the venue.

### RETREATS

Attendance for the entire retreat (excluding day 0) is mandatory. We also strive for a mini-retreat when possible.

A retreat counts as a forum meeting, with the same roles and norms in force.

A retreat committee of 3, led by the retreat chair, organises the retreat.

The annual retreat budget is estimated at SGD 5,000 per person by default.

The budget is all inclusive (accommodation, food and drinks, all shared costs and activities including entertainment), except for flights.

As a best practice, retreat committee will check for YEN resources to enable a good retreat.

Budget revision follows YEN best practice: the moderator or retreat chair collects everyone's preference anonymously (paper or anonymous poll); the lowest figure at or above the previous budget is set as the limit.

Once retreat dates are confirmed, changes require unanimous agreement at least 60 calendar days before the new date. By default, the person who created the change in date takes over as retreat chair.

Participation in all official retreat activities (once the itinerary is signed off) is mandatory and inclusive. Official retreat hours runs until end of daily dinner.

Showing up for or participating in drinking etc, or extreme activities, and anything with potential safety and religious infringements, are always optional and paid for separately by participants.

### DOING BUSINESS TOGETHER

Forum members do not engage in material business with each other without the prior unanimous consent of the forum.

Materiality threshold is SGD100,000 per fiscal year for any private market transactions and must be disclosed to the forum followed by a unanimous transaction approval vote from all forum members.

If consent is not granted and the member pursues the opportunity anyway, the member must exit the forum first prior to any exchange of funds.

Co-investments controlled and managed by a third party are permitted though discouraged; equal opportunity must be offered to all members, and the consent and solicitation rules above still apply.

### FORUM HEALTH AND RENEWAL

We use the Forum Health Survey annually to diagnose the group's health. ▸ The moderator is responsible for running the survey.

We bring in Certified Forum Facilitators at least once every 3 years.

The moderator reviews norms at each annual retreat.

### FISCAL YEAR

Dues collected: SGD200/pax per year collected at start of fiscal year

Attendance is measured over the calendar year (January to December).

Proposed: dues are collected each January, amount set by the treasurer's budget for the year.

Elected positions hand over in January, alongside the blind vote.

### Amendment

Any proposed amendment to the Forum Norms requires unanimous written consent from all members.
